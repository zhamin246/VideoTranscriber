## 3. Service Description

imagetocad is an **AI converter** described on our landing page. Depending on the features you use, the Service may:

- Accept a **photo, sketch, logo, signature, or similar file** (JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, or PDF, as allowed)
- Generate **semantic CAD linework** (not a pixel auto-trace)
- Return the same drawing as **DXF, SVG, and PDF**, with millimetre units (default longest edge about 300 mm — you rescale if you know a real length)
- Provide related tools on the site (for example Image to CAD)
- Save **conversion history** when you are signed in
- Offer **3 free conversions** to start, then **credit packs** or **Hobby / Pro / Studio** subscriptions as shown on Pricing

**Important**: Output is a **starting point** — editable 2D linework for CAD, laser, vinyl, or design. It is **not** a sealed construction document, a parametric 3D assembly, a BIM model, or a substitute for a licensed professional. You must review, scale, and finish the drawing in your own tools. The same photo can produce small differences between runs; this is generative drawing, not a deterministic trace.
