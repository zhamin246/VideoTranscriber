## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **imagetocad**, the AI converter at **imagetocad.app**. imagetocad turns a photo, sketch, or similar file into **CAD-ready vector linework** you can download as **DXF, SVG, or PDF** — for CAD, laser, and design. It is a starting point you review and finish in your own tools, not a sealed construction document.

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have when you use imagetocad (the “Service”). By using the Service, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

This Policy is written to match how imagetocad is described on our landing page: upload a photo, AI reads the subject, you get editable linework in about a minute, with **3 free conversions** to start.

## 4. Information We Collect

### 4.1 Information you provide

- **Account information** (when you sign in): name, email address, password or auth-provider identifiers (for example Google or GitHub), and similar profile details you submit.
- **Uploads**: photos, sketches, logos, signatures, PDFs, or image URLs you submit for conversion (JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, or PDF, as allowed by the product).
- **Communications**: messages you send to support at **support@imagetocad.app**, including attachments.
- **Purchase details**: product selected (credit packs or Hobby / Pro / Studio plans), order identifiers, and billing metadata needed to fulfill payment. **Payment card numbers are processed by our payment provider(s)** (for example Stripe); we do not store full card numbers on our servers when the provider supports that model.

### 4.2 Information generated by the Service

- **Conversion outputs**: line-art previews, vector geometry, and downloadable **DXF, SVG, and PDF** files generated from your upload.
- **Job records**: conversion status, titles, timestamps, credit use, and links to stored originals, linework, and geometry for history on your dashboard.
- **Technical logs**: processing parameters, success/failure of a job, and flags needed to run and debug conversions.

### 4.3 Information collected automatically

- **Usage data**: pages viewed, features used, approximate timestamps, referral URLs, and interaction events that help us understand product performance.
- **Device and browser data**: device type, OS, browser type/version, language, screen or viewport signals, and IP address (or truncated IP where we apply privacy controls).
- **Cookies and similar technologies**: session cookies, preference cookies, and analytics cookies where enabled (for example Google Analytics). You can control cookies through your browser; some features may not work without essential cookies.

### 4.4 Information we do **not** intentionally collect

- We do not require sensitive categories such as race, religion, or health diagnoses to convert an image.
- We do not ask for government ID numbers for standard conversion.
- imagetocad outputs are **drafting aids**, not certified drawings, BIM models, or professional engineering stamps.

## 5. How We Use Information

We use information to:

1. **Provide the core product** — accept your upload, run AI linework generation, vectorize the drawing, and return DXF, SVG, and PDF as described on the landing page.
2. **Operate free conversions and paid credits** — grant the **3 free conversions** to start, then debit credits or subscription allotments for further jobs.
3. **Save history when you are signed in** — keep originals, linework, and files so Conversion History and re-downloads work.
4. **Operate accounts and checkout** — authenticate you, process payments, prevent fraud, and send receipts.
5. **Improve reliability** — fix bugs, measure conversion success, and improve speed and quality **using aggregated or de-identified data where practical**.
6. **Communicate with you** — respond to support, send transactional messages (for example purchase confirmation), and, only if allowed by law and your preferences, product updates.
7. **Secure the Service** — detect abuse, spam, or unauthorized access.
8. **Comply with law** — meet legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.

We process uploads **to convert the file you requested**. We **do not sell** your photos. We **do not** use your photos to train **public** AI models **without your consent**.

## 6. Photo and Conversion Processing

### 6.1 What happens when you convert

- Conversion **requires sending your image to our servers** (and to cloud AI providers we use to generate linework). This is not an on-device-only product.
- We store the original, the line-art preview, and vector geometry in object storage so we can finish the job, show a before/after preview, and let signed-in users reopen history.
- Sample images on the marketing site are our demonstration assets, not your uploads.

### 6.2 Free conversions

- Your first **3 conversions** are free; no card is required to try.
- To **save drawings** to Conversion History, we ask you to **sign in**. Unsigned jobs may not persist after the session.

### 6.3 Paid use

- Further conversions use **credits** (packs that expire in 12 months, or monthly/yearly plans as shown on Pricing).
- Paid account history stays available while your account is active, subject to Section 8.

### 6.4 What we do **not** do with your uploads by default

- We **do not sell** your photos or drawings.
- We **do not** use your uploads to train **public** AI models **without your consent**.
- We do not publish your files publicly unless **you** choose to share a download or link.

## 15. Automated Processing

Linework is **generative drawing**, not a deterministic pixel trace. The same photo can produce small differences between runs. Outputs are **2D editable vectors** for you to review, dimension, or cut — not a substitute for a full CAD model, BIM, or a sealed construction document. We do not use conversions to make legal or similarly significant decisions about you (credit, employment, insurance, etc.).
