## 2. Free Conversions

Your first **3 conversions** are provided **at no charge**. There is nothing to refund for free usage.

## 3. Failed Conversions (Automatic Credit Refund)

If a paid conversion **fails** on our side after a credit was deducted, we **return that credit automatically**. You do not need to email us for an ordinary failed job.

A **retry** of a successful or cancelled job uses another credit. That is not a failure refund.

## 4. Credit Packs

Credit packs (Starter, Popular, Plus, Bulk, or similar) allocate compute as soon as they are issued.

- Pack credits are **generally non-refundable** once purchased, because resources are allocated immediately.  
- Exceptions: **required by law**; a **verified platform failure** that prevented any use of the pack; or **clear duplicate / accidental charges** we can confirm.  
- Unused credits **expire 12 months** after purchase and have no cash value after expiry.

If you have **not used any credit** from a pack, contact us within **7 days** of purchase at **support@imagetocad.app**. We may refund or cancel that unused pack at our reasonable discretion, except where law requires a refund.

## 5. Subscriptions (Hobby, Pro, Studio)

- You may **cancel anytime**. You keep the remaining allowance until the current billing period ends.  
- We **do not refund previous subscription usage** (time or credits already granted and available in a period that has started).  
- **First charge only**: if you subscribe, use **no conversions**, and cancel within **7 days** of the initial charge, contact **support@imagetocad.app**. We may refund that first charge at our reasonable discretion, except where law requires a refund.  
- Already-elapsed periods, granted credits you have used, and payment-network fees may not be recoverable. Accidental yearly-vs-monthly purchases may be converted or partially refunded at our reasonable discretion.

Yearly plans drip credits monthly. Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not automatically cash out unused monthly allotments.

## 6. Non-Refundable or Limited Situations

We may deny or limit a refund when:

- The request is made **more than 7 days** after purchase (unless a longer period is required by law)  
- Credits from the purchase have **already been used**  
- We reasonably detect **fraud**, chargeback abuse, or repeated refund farming  
- You request a refund for a product that was **never purchased** or already fully refunded  
- Local law requires a different outcome (we will follow applicable law)  

## 7. How to Request a Refund

Email **[support@imagetocad.app](mailto:support@imagetocad.app)** with:

1. The email used at checkout (or account email)  
2. Order number, transaction ID, or approximate purchase date  
3. Product name (for example “Popular pack” or “Pro yearly”)  
4. Brief reason (optional but helpful)  

### Processing time  
We typically review requests within **1–3 business days** and process approved refunds within **5–7 business days**. Banks and card networks may take longer to show the credit.

Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** when possible. Credit-only adjustments (failed jobs) appear in your credit balance, not as a card refund.

## 8. Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a chargeback so we can resolve the issue quickly. Unwarranted chargebacks may lead to account restrictions. Valid refund requests under this Policy do not require a chargeback.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy as products or laws change. Updates will be posted on this page with a new **Last Updated** date. For purchases already completed, the policy in effect at purchase generally applies, except where a change is more favorable to you or required by law.
