# Design System: image to cad

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The AudioCleaner converter well"**

The homepage follows the AudioCleaner AI landing grammar: a lilac promo bar, frosted white header, centered H1, and a large dashed upload well as the first action. Product facts stay image to cad (photo → DXF / SVG / PDF). Do not invent user volume.

**Key Characteristics:**
- White paper + lilac `#8882F5`
- Lexend 700 display, 16px body
- 12–16px radii, not burgundy pills
- Hero is the dropzone, not a split photo

## Colors

Restrained: neutrals plus one lilac family.

- **Lilac** `#8882F5` — promo bar, CTAs, after badges
- **Hover** `#726BE8`
- **Drop well** `#5C56B8` → `#3E3A86`
- **Ink** `#111827`
- **Mute** `#606266`
- **Paper** `#FFFFFF` / `#F7F6FF`

## Typography

Lexend for marketing chrome. Inter remains the app fallback.

No Instrument Serif italic on this surface.

## Layout

Content ~1106px. Sticky header ~56px. Promo bar 44px. Alternate white, pale lilac, and deep indigo wells.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the upload well as the first viewport action.
- Do not invent 30M files or 5M users.
- Do not restore burgundy/rose Face Report tokens on the home page.
